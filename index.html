Luminous Music Player

featured project

A high-performance home for the music you already own.

Luminous is a fast, local-first player for your own audio library — no streaming, no subscriptions, no cloud. Just your files, indexed, searchable, and beautifully played.

Built with Rust, Tauri v2, TypeScript, and Svelte 5 (Runes), it offers a lightweight, premium experience on both Windows and Linux desktops with a beautiful dynamic user interface.
