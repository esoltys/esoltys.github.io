Features

Get to know the artists you love.

Open an artist and their story is already there: a bio, logos and artwork, and any shows coming up. Luminous gathers it all from MusicBrainz and fanart.tv, and the info sidebar is always one click away.

Sing along, line by line.

Lyrics follow the song as it plays. If the timing is a little off, nudge it right there. And if a song has no lyrics yet, you can add and sync your own.

Keep the music going while you get on with your day.

Tuck Luminous into a small miniplayer that floats above your other windows. Skip a track, glance at the lyrics, or pause, without opening the whole app.

Let your playlists take care of themselves.

Make a smart playlist from rules you choose, or start with the ready-made ones for decades, genres, and tempo. They stay up to date as your collection grows.

Curious what you've been listening to?

See your listening streak, find out what time of day you reach for music, and look back at your favourite artists and albums from the past week, month, or year.

Help your headphones sound their best.

Drag the bands right on the graph, type in exact numbers, or load a ready-made AutoEq profile for your headphones. There are up to 20 bands if you like to tinker.

Dress it up to match your mood.

Pick a theme you like, or switch on the dynamic theme and Luminous borrows its colours from each album's cover art, so the whole player feels like part of the music.

Find that one song, however you remember it.

Type something like genre:rock to narrow your whole library in one line. It works with details like key and BPM too, and any search can become a smart playlist.

Sort your genres the way you think about them.

Drag tags between genre cards to build your own sub-genres, like Shoegaze under Alternative or Synthwave under Electronic.

Show your favourites some love.

Tap the heart on songs you love (right-click it for the ones you don't), and give them 1 to 5 stars separately. Sign in with MusicBrainz and your loves and ratings sync with ListenBrainz both ways.

Fix the little details.

Correct a title, artist, or album right from the track, with no extra tools. Edit one song at a time or many at once, or hand it to MusicBrainz Picard to look things up for you.

A tidy library, without the housework.

Pick a folder naming template and Luminous files your music to match. Turn on auto-organize and new songs and tag changes find their place as they arrive.

Get Luminous

Download Luminous and turn your folder of files into a real music library.

Windows

Windows 10 & 11 — installs and updates automatically.

  • Portable ZIP No installer — keep Luminous and its data together, even on a USB drive.

Linux

Add repo & install

Linux — add the esoltys.dev repo once, then updates automatically.

  • .deb package Debian, Ubuntu, and derivatives. Updated via your package manager.
  • .rpm package Fedora, openSUSE, and derivatives. Updated via your package manager.
  • AppImage Runs on most distributions — download, make it executable, and play.
